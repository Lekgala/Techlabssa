# TechLabs Academy SA User Guide

This guide is for applicants and students using the TechLabs Academy SA website and secure student portal. Staff members should use [STAFF_GUIDE.md](STAFF_GUIDE.md).

## 1. What you can do in the system

You can use the website to:

- explore courses, curriculum, labs, pricing, intakes, and career support;
- apply for an available course and cohort;
- track your application and hardware review;
- receive and view an invoice;
- pay by EFT and submit proof of payment (POP);
- view your cohort calendar, course modules, and practical support tickets;
- download student documents and verified payment receipts;
- contact admissions or enrolled-student support; and
- view or independently verify a Certificate of Completion.

TechLabs provides independent, non-accredited practical skills training. A TechLabs Certificate of Completion is not a university qualification or vendor certification.

## 2. Before you apply

Review the following website sections before starting an application:

- **Courses and Curriculum:** compare the available course tiers and module content.
- **Labs and How It Works:** understand the practical lab and incident-resolution approach.
- **Pricing:** review tuition, deposit, full-payment, and instalment options.
- **Intakes:** check cohort dates, delivery format, location, and available seats.
- **Laptop Requirements:** confirm that your Windows laptop can run the required VMware labs.
- **Terms, Privacy, and Refund Policy:** read the declarations that apply to your application and enrollment.

Use an email address and WhatsApp number that you check regularly. Admissions decisions, account links, payment updates, and other important notices are sent to your registered details.

## 3. Submit an application

Select **Apply** or **Apply for Next Intake**. The application has seven steps:

1. **Personal Information:** enter your legal name, email address, WhatsApp number, city or suburb, and province.
2. **IT Background:** provide your qualification, experience, employment status, current role, and relevant background.
3. **Laptop Specifications:** provide accurate operating system, processor, memory, storage, free-space, and virtualisation information.
4. **Course Tier:** select the training package you want.
5. **Cohort:** select an intake that is accepting applications.
6. **Consent:** read and accept the required declarations and policies.
7. **Summary:** check every answer, then submit the application.

Required fields are marked with an asterisk. You can return to an earlier completed step before submission. Review your email address carefully because it becomes your portal username.

After submission:

- save your application reference number for payments and support queries;
- check your email, including the junk or spam folder;
- allow admissions to review your application and laptop information; and
- do not submit a duplicate application merely because a decision is still pending.

Admissions normally communicates the outcome by email. Approval does not reserve a seat by itself; your required payment must be received and verified while capacity remains available.

## 4. Create and access your student account

If your application is approved, use the secure account link in the approval email to create a password. The password must contain at least 10 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a number.

To sign in:

1. Open **Student Login**.
2. Enter the same email address used in your application.
3. Enter your password and select **Sign In**.

Your application reference is not a password and cannot be used to sign in.

### Forgot your password

Select **Forgot Password**, enter your registered email address, and request a recovery link. Open the email and set a new password. Secure links expire and can be used only once; request a new link if one is invalid or has already been used.

### Sign in without a password

Select **Email Me a Login Link**, enter your registered email address, and use the one-time link sent to you.

If you were approved before student accounts were introduced, use **Forgot Password** to create access. Never share your password or one-time login link.

## 5. Track your application

The **Overview** tab shows your reference number, current stage, hardware review, payment requirement, portal-access state, admissions feedback, and recommended next step.

Common stages include:

- **Under review / New:** admissions has received the application.
- **Hardware review:** your laptop information is being checked.
- **Approved / Payment required:** you may view the invoice, banking details, and payment action.
- **POP submitted:** your proof of payment is awaiting comparison with the academy bank statement.
- **Enrolled:** the required payment was verified and a cohort seat was available.
- **Waitlisted:** a seat is not currently available; follow the instructions from admissions.
- **Completed:** course completion has been recorded.

The wording shown in the portal is the current status of your own record. Contact admissions if the status or feedback needs clarification.

## 6. Make a payment and upload POP

Banking details become available only after admissions approves your application and creates an invoice. Always use the details displayed in your own portal rather than details copied from an old message or another student.

1. Open **Payments** or select the payment action on **Overview**.
2. Review the invoice number, tuition, amount paid, remaining balance, and instalment schedule where applicable.
3. Pay by EFT to the displayed account.
4. Use the invoice number shown by the portal as the EFT reference.
5. Enter the reference in the **EFT payment reference** field.
6. Select one clear, bank-generated POP file.
7. Select **Submit proof for verification**.

Accepted POP formats are PDF, JPG, and PNG, with a maximum file size of 5 MB.

An uploaded POP is only a submission for review. It does not confirm that the academy received the money. Do not upload the same POP again while its status is **Submitted** or **Awaiting Verification**. Admissions verifies the transaction against the bank statement and then updates your paid total and balance.

If a POP is rejected, read the reason, correct the issue, and submit the requested replacement. If a verified deposit is sufficient but the selected cohort is full, the system may place the application on the waitlist rather than enroll it.

## 7. Use the student portal

The portal tabs may scroll horizontally on a small screen.

### Overview

Use **Overview** as your starting point. It shows your next action, next lesson, curriculum progress, active practical tickets, and application information when enrollment is not yet complete.

### Calendar

Use **Calendar** to view induction and scheduled module lessons. Use the arrow buttons to move between months and consult **Schedule details** for the full event information. The calendar appears after a cohort is assigned and updates if cohort dates or the published curriculum change.

### Modules

Use **Modules** to view module descriptions, technologies, scheduled dates, current work, and completed work. Detailed learning resources remain locked until your status is **Enrolled**.

### Payments

Use **Payments** to view the official invoice, tuition total, paid-to-date amount, outstanding balance, instalment schedule, current payment status, EFT details, and POP upload form. Verified payments appear separately from pending submissions.

### Documents

Use **Documents** to open available records:

- the official invoice after it is created;
- the course schedule after cohort assignment;
- submitted POP records after upload;
- payment receipts after verification;
- admission confirmation after enrollment; and
- the certificate after successful completion and issue.

Some documents contain personal and financial information. Download them only to a trusted device and do not post them publicly.

### Tickets

Practical tickets simulate real IT support incidents. Open an assigned ticket, investigate the issue in the approved lab environment, and document the root cause, corrective action, and verification steps. Select **Submit & Mark Ticket Resolved** only when the work and notes are complete.

Do not use academy lab access or tools for unauthorised testing, scanning, scraping, or activity outside the assigned educational environment.

### Certificate

The **Certificate** tab displays your Certificate of Completion after it has been issued. Check that your name, course, completion date, grade, and certificate number are correct before sharing it.

## 8. Get support

Before enrollment, use the admissions contact or WhatsApp action and include your application reference. After enrollment, use **Student Support Chat** in the portal; its message automatically includes your identity, reference, and cohort. If your cohort has a Teams channel, use **Open Teams Channel** for approved cohort communication.

When requesting help, provide:

- your full name and application reference;
- the page or portal tab involved;
- the exact error message;
- what you were trying to do; and
- a screenshot that does not expose a password, login link, bank credentials, or unrelated personal data.

Never email or send anyone your password, secure access token, or the system `.env` file.

## 9. Verify a certificate

Anyone can use the public **Verify Certificate** page:

1. Enter the full certificate number exactly as shown.
2. Select **Verify Certificate**.
3. Compare the returned name, course, completion date, and grade with the certificate presented.

A **Certificate Not Found** result can mean the number is incorrect or no matching certificate has been issued. Contact the academy through its official details if confirmation is required.

## 10. Troubleshooting

### I did not receive an email

Check the email address used in your application, then check junk, spam, promotions, and blocked-sender folders. Wait a few minutes before requesting another access message. Contact admissions if the registered email address is wrong or delivery continues to fail.

### My secure link does not work

Links expire and work once. Request a fresh password-recovery or email-login link and use only the newest message.

### I cannot sign in

Use your registered email address, not the application reference. Check for typing errors, then use **Forgot Password**. If the problem continues, contact admissions.

### I cannot see banking details or upload a POP

Payment access is available only when the application has been approved and an invoice exists. Check the current stage and next action on **Overview**.

### My POP is still pending

Admissions must verify it against the bank statement. Do not submit it again. Check the portal and email for the decision or a request for corrected evidence.

### My payment is verified but I am waitlisted

The cohort had no available seat when enrollment was processed. Contact admissions about an available intake or an authorised cohort transfer; do not make another payment unless instructed.

### A document is unavailable

Each document has a prerequisite. For example, a receipt requires a verified payment, admission confirmation requires enrollment, and a certificate requires successful completion and issue.

### My personal or course information is incorrect

Contact admissions or student support. Staff must make audited corrections; do not create a second application to fix an existing record.

## 11. Safe use and privacy

- Sign out after using a shared or public computer.
- Keep your email account and portal password secure.
- Confirm the invoice number and displayed banking details before every EFT.
- Do not share POPs, receipts, invoices, grades, or attendance records unnecessarily.
- Use only official TechLabs website, email, WhatsApp, and cohort channels.
- Report suspicious login messages, altered banking details, duplicate payment requests, or unexplained balance changes immediately.
- Review the website's current Terms, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy for the rules that govern your application and enrollment.
